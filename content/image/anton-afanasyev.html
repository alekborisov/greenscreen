Антон Афанасьев. The path to freedom. Алматы, Казахстан
This image was born from the feeling of a person's inner freedom and the circumstances in which he found himself. When the endless sky is above your head, it seems that you can grab a bundle of balloons and fly high into the sky. Below, you will find the hardships and insignificant people with their petty struggles. In contrast to the bright balloons, one of the symbols of Russia is the gray concrete fences with hostile barbed wire, which also symbolize a solitary cell. The dandelion lawn symbolizes a carefree and naive childhood, a sense of homeland that is untouched by the world of adults. Oh, if only it were possible! Freedom for political prisoners!
