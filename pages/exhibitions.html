Greenscreen exhibitions exist simultaneously within physical and digital environments.
Green Screen as a physical chroma-key surface is an open interface that allows an artwork and an exhibition to move beyond their given form. It can take the form of a gallery space, an outdoor surface, an object, or a situation.
The website greenscreen.gallery is not a reflection of the gallery, but its networked extension, operating simultaneously with the physical exhibition. Here, an exhibition can acquire additional contexts and connections, while artworks can take on new forms of presence.
Each Greenscreen exhibition forms its own configuration of relationships between artists, artworks, viewers, environments, and other material and digital agents. The physical and networked layers can therefore interact in different ways: the website can continue the exhibition, show what is happening in the gallery, reveal additional materials, or become an independent part of the exhibition experience.
